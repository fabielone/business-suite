## Writing a useful request
- **Where:** screen or step, for example "Pipeline card" or "Releasing an Order".
- **Who:** the role that is affected.
- **Now:** what happens today.
- **Wanted:** what should happen instead.
- A phone photo or screenshot with fictional data helps more than a long description.
